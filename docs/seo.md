# NearGrow Ads — SEO & Search Architecture

## 1. Technical SEO Implementation

- **Semantic HTML5**: Native `<header>`, `<nav>`, `<main>`, `<section>`, `<article>`, `<aside>`, and `<footer>` elements.
- **Heading Hierarchy**: Exactly one `<h1>` per page, followed by logical `<h2>` and `<h3>` section divisions.
- **Metadata**: Unique `<title>` and `<meta name="description">` on the index HTML and structured JSON-LD Schema.org graphs.
- **Sitemap**: Clean, verified `/sitemap.xml` referencing all 8 service landing pages, 4 educational articles, and core agency routes.
- **Robots Directives**: `/robots.txt` specifying open crawl permissions and the sitemap canonical reference.

## 2. Structured Data (JSON-LD)

Implemented schemas:
- `Organization`: Identity, name, slogan ("Building Brands Together"), and published India office address.
- `WebSite`: Publisher reference and official URL.
- Keep factual office/contact details distinct from service-market positioning; do not imply unverified offices or service locations.

## 3. Market-Aware Search Strategy

- Avoidance of thin, duplicate doorway pages.
- Position service content around international and multi-location growth while retaining accurate, specific geography in documented case studies and the published office address.
- Transparent non-guarantee disclosures complying with search engine quality evaluator guidelines.
